Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Donut Tag V1 ("the game") is a free multiplayer virtual reality game made by Donnut Studios, the independent developer hubeeerrk ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who processes it, and what you can do about it.

Short version: we use a random game account ID, the nickname you pick and your in-game items so the game works. Voice chat is sent live to other players in your room and we do not record or store it. We do not sell your data and the game has no ads.

The game does not ask for your real name, address, phone number, payment details or precise location. It does not use advertising or tracking for ads.

Microphone

The game asks for microphone access only so you can talk to other players in voice chat. You can deny the permission or mute yourself in game and still play. Audio is streamed live and is never saved by us.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the game. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: game accounts, nicknames, items and bans.
  • Photon by Exit Games: multiplayer rooms and voice chat.
  • Meta: the Meta Horizon Store and the Quest platform you downloaded the game from. Meta processes your Meta account data under its own policy, not ours.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. They use legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for those transfers.

Legal basis (EEA and UK players)

We process the information above to provide the game you asked to play (performance of a contract) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe, fair and working (moderation, security and bug fixing).

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Other players can see your nickname, your character and hear your voice when you use voice chat. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of players.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older, in line with Meta's account requirements. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Voice chat lets players talk to people they do not know, so parents should review the game and the Quest family settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. If you are in the EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). See Delete my data for how to make a request.

Security

Your data is stored with the service providers listed above, who use industry security measures. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not share personal information in your nickname or in voice chat.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in game or on our community channels.

Delete my data

You can ask us to delete your Donut Tag V1 account and everything linked to it: your nickname, items, currency and moderation history except where we must keep a record for safety.

  1. Send an email to the address below with the subject Delete my Donut Tag account.
  2. Include your in-game nickname and, if you can, the player ID shown on the in-game computer.
  3. We confirm your request and delete the account within 30 days.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account by itself. After deletion, starting the game again creates a new, empty account.
